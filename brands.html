Mirror & glass

Mirror Image

Mirror Image takes the custom process to new levels. With order quantities of one, they are able to build decorative mirrors to the exact design, size, and finish a project calls for. Their line runs from framed and frameless to LED mirrors with dimming, defogging, and touch controls, along with glass shower enclosures, bath hardware/accessories, and decorative architectural glass. They are able to work with budgets and have mirrors in hotels ranging from one to five stars! Endless possibilities for unique guest rooms, restaurants, and high-end homes.

Reclaimed teak

IndoTeak

IndoTeak reclaims old-growth teak from buildings slated for demolition in Indonesia and gives it a second life as flooring, wall tile, decking, siding, CNC panels, yacht flooring, millwork, and stair risers. The wood is 100% reclaimed and FSC certified. It brings the rich color, tight grain, and natural durability of timber that's had decades, sometimes centuries, to mature. IndoTeak can customize finish, color, pattern, and more to your custom design needs.

  • Engineered & Solid Flooring
  • Wall Tile & Mosaics
  • Decking
  • Siding & Paneling
  • Stairs & Treads
  • Custom Pieces
  • CNC Capabilities
  • Vintage Sourcing
Best for
Hospitality, retail, and residential interiors and exteriors
Finishes
Natural matte, patina, sand, cognac, tobacco, or unfinished
