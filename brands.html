Mirror & glass

Mirror Image

For over 30 years, Mirror Image has manufactured high-quality custom mirror and glass products for the hospitality, restaurant, and luxury residential sectors. Their specialties include illuminated and framed mirrors, glass doors, shower enclosures, and decorative glass.

While known for producing products for guestrooms and multifamily projects, they offer flexibility with no minimum order quantities on most items, whether you select and customize a product from their standard line or create a bespoke design to fit your aesthetic and budget. They work on projects of all types and sizes, ranging from limited-service and extended-stay properties to full-service and luxury hotels. Mirror Image is proud to be a preferred vendor for leading brands such as Hilton, Marriott, IHG, Wynn Resorts, MGM Resorts International, and many more.

Reclaimed teak

IndoTeak

IndoTeak reclaims old-growth teak from buildings slated for demolition across Indonesia and gives it a second life in hospitality, restaurant, and luxury residential spaces. Their specialties include engineered and solid flooring, wall tile and mosaics, decking, siding, CNC panels, yacht flooring, millwork, and stair risers.

Every board is 100% reclaimed and FSC certified, bringing the rich color, tight grain, and natural durability of timber that has had decades, sometimes centuries, to mature. IndoTeak can customize finish, color, and pattern to fit your design, from natural matte and patina to deep cognac and tobacco tones. IndoTeak is featured in Starbucks Reserve Headquarters and hotel brands such as W, Fairmont, Nobu, and luxury residences all over the world.
