Mirror & glass

Mirror Image

Mirror Image builds decorative mirrors and glass to the exact size and finish a project calls for. Their line runs from framed and frameless mirrors to LED mirrors with dimming, defogging, and touch controls, along with shower enclosures, bath accessories, and decorative architectural glass. You'll find it in hotel guest rooms, restaurant restrooms, and high-end homes.

  • Custom Mirrors
  • LED / Lit Mirrors
  • Framed & Frameless
  • Shower Enclosures
  • Architectural Glass
  • Bath Accessories

Reclaimed teak

Indoteak Design

Indoteak Design reclaims old-growth teak from buildings slated for demolition in Indonesia and gives it a second life as flooring, wall tile, decking, siding, and stairs. The wood is 100% reclaimed and FSC certified. It brings the rich color, tight grain, and natural durability of timber that's had decades, sometimes centuries, to mature.

  • Engineered & Solid Flooring
  • Wall Tile & Mosaics
  • Decking
  • Siding & Paneling
  • Stairs & Treads
  • Custom Pieces
Best for
Hospitality, retail, and residential interiors and exteriors
Finishes
Natural matte, patina, sand, cognac, tobacco, or unfinished
Sustainability
100% reclaimed, FSC-certified teak
