What we do

Independent reps sit between the maker and the people who specify. We carry a small, focused line so we can know every product well.

Bilkis & Co. represents manufacturers to architects, interior designers, contractors, purchasing agents, and hospitality groups across the New York metro area and Philadelphia. We bring samples to your studio, help size and specify product, pull pricing and lead times, and keep the order moving until it's installed.

Because we're independent, we only take on brands we'd put in our own projects. Right now that means two: Mirror Image, for custom mirrors and glass, and Indoteak Design, for reclaimed teak.
