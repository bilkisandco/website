About us

Built On Relationships

What we do

Bilkis & Co., founded by Seth Bilkis, represents manufacturers to the hospitality and residential design community across the New York metro area. We provide quick communication, samples on hand, and knowledgeable advice when it comes to customization.

Seth studied Interior Design at the Savannah College of Art and Design. He worked in design before realizing his true passion was working with the people and materials themselves. He founded his first business, Matchbox 912, selling vintage matchbook collage artwork for more than three years before moving to New York City.

In NYC, he worked at a design collective as lead sales representative for 10 brands, making meaningful connections with designers at hospitality firms all around the city.

Now he has partnered with Mirror Image and IndoTeak to start a new chapter of their representation in NYC, and he's excited and eager to work with you!
